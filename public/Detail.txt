About Us:
At Restore Aid Foundation, we are dedicated to serving crisis-affected Christians in Nigerian communities. Our mission is to provide holistic support, empowerment, and training to help individuals and communities rebuild their lives, grow in faith, and thrive.

What We Do:
- Feeding: Providing emergency food assistance to individuals and families in crisis, ensuring they have access to nutritious food and can focus on rebuilding their lives.
- Housing: Offering temporary shelter and housing support to those who have lost their homes due to crisis, providing a safe and stable environment as they work towards recovery.
- Health: Providing medical care and treatment through our network of volunteer nurses and doctors, who conduct tests, diagnose conditions, and administer medication and treatment to those in need.
- Discipleship Growth Program (DGP): Nurturing the spiritual growth and development of crisis-affected individuals through Bible-based discipleship, prayer, and mentorship.
- Skill Acquisition Program: Equipping individuals with practical skills and training to enhance their employability, entrepreneurship, and self-sufficiency, empowering them to rebuild their lives and contribute to their communities.

Restore Aid Foundations was established with a passion to make a meaningful difference in the lives of those affected by crisis. We believe that every individual deserves hope, dignity, and purpose, and we are committed to providing the support and resources needed to achieve these goals.

Why Nigeria?

Nigeria is a key focus due to its complex crisis landscape and the disproportionate impact on vulnerable populations, including Christians in Northern Nigeria. With over 200 million people, Nigeria faces various crises, such as Boko Haram insurgency, banditry, and communal clashes, resulting in widespread displacement, violence, and human rights abuses. According to UNICEF, the country has one of the highest numbers of out-of-school children and high rates of child malnutrition. Christians in Northern Nigeria face significant challenges, including violence, displacement, and marginalization. Our organization aims to provide holistic support, empowerment, and training to help individuals and communities rebuild their lives, grow in faith, and become resilient in the face of adversity. By working in Nigeria, we seek to make a meaningful difference in the lives of crisis-affected Christians and contribute to the country's stability and development.
